What It Is

Not a Picture Day.
A Production.

The Phireworx Media Day Experience is a prepaid, team-funded shoot where the organization covers the cost upfront — not parents, not individual ordering. No volume sales model. No assembly-line photos. No pay-per-image.

Instead, every final image is delivered in full resolution to the team. Your program walks away with a complete library of high-impact visuals ready to use anywhere — social, print, flyers, recruiting, press coverage.

Advanced Production

Smoke machines, colored gels, dramatic lighting setups — effects not feasible in high-volume picture day workflows.

Full Digital Ownership

Every edited image from the shoot is delivered to you. Complete usage rights. Use them however, whenever, for anything.

Flexible Locations

Gyms, fields, custom setups, or off-site environments. We build the look around your team's environment and identity.
