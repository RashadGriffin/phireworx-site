Founder

Rashad Griffin

Growing up in Phoenix, Rashad Griffin was three things at once: a creator, a youth worker, and a lifelong sports fan. By 2012, he'd been producing music, designing visually, and working directly with youth through parks and recreation and elementary schools — while staying deeply connected to the games he'd always loved.

The epiphany hit when he realized picture day for young athletes was broken. Dull, rushed, and forgettable — nothing like the energy those kids brought to the field every game. He saw a void and a mission in the same moment.

Phireworx Photography was created to change that — to bring elite-level creative production, real engagement with young athletes, and a process that made coaches' lives easier, not harder. Twelve years and hundreds of Arizona programs later, that mission hasn't changed.

01
The Creator

From music production to visual design, Rashad brings a genuine creative eye to every shoot. The dramatic lighting, the compositing, the banner graphics — it's all art, not assembly.

02
The Youth Worker

Years working with kids in parks and schools means Rashad knows how to connect with young athletes. Picture day isn't stressful — it's energetic, fun, and the athletes actually look forward to it.

03
The Athlete

As a lifelong sports fan and athlete, Rashad understands what it means to compete. That authenticity shows in photos where athletes look powerful, not posed.
