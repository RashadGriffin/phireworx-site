Important Information

Session Policy

Team Photo Day Sessions

There is no minimum order required if we are already photographing your program's team and individual photos. Banner orders can be added to your existing Photo Day session at no additional session fee.

Banner-Only Sessions

A minimum banner order of $600 is required for banner-only sessions. If your total order is below $600, you will be invoiced the difference to meet the minimum.

A $10 shipping charge applies to all banner orders under $350.
