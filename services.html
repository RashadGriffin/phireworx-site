Team Media Day
Experience

A completely different product from Picture Day. The Media Day Experience is a prepaid, creative-first production shoot where the organization — not parents — funds the session. Every edited digital from the shoot is delivered directly to the team. No ordering portal. No packages. No per-photo fees.

Advanced lighting setups, flexible locations, and more time per athlete for intentional, personality-driven shots. Built for programs that want high-impact content for social, branding, recruiting, and promotions.
